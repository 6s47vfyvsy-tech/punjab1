Research notes

Sources & photo credits

This educational project presents Punjab as a historic region now divided between India and Pakistan. It begins with the high period of the Sikh Empire and follows political, social and cultural change to 2026. Partition and late-20th-century conflicts remain deeply personal and contested; readers should compare scholarly perspectives and oral histories.

Technical note

The site uses Wikimedia Commons file redirects, so photos require an internet connection. To make the repository fully offline, download the credited files into assets/images/ and replace the remote image URLs in index.html.
